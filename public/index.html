How it works

Discover, learn, practise, complete

The same clear path in every course, so you always know what to do next.

  1. 1
    Discover your course

    Pick your university, faculty and programme — StudyCore shows only the courses you actually study.

  2. 2
    Select a topic

    Topics group the lessons, notes and papers for one part of the syllabus, with progress on each.

  3. 3
    Read and watch

    Notes open in the StudyCore document viewer; video lessons play in the StudyCore player with resume.

  4. 4
    Practise and track

    Work past papers, mark lessons complete, and watch your streak and course completion grow.
