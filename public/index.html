Questions

Before you sign up

The things students ask us on WhatsApp most often.

What do I get for free?

Every new account starts with a 30-day free trial. During the trial you can read all study notes, tutorial sheets and past papers for your program's courses, and track your progress. Video lessons are Premium content, so they stay locked until you upgrade.

How much is Premium and how do I pay?

Premium is K50 for 30 days. You pay by mobile money — MTN MoMo or Airtel Money — to the number shown on the Pricing page, then submit your payment reference from your dashboard.

The StudyCore team confirms the payment and Premium activates on your account. You get an email as soon as access is granted. No card and no merchant account needed.

Why do I only see some of the courses?

StudyCore is program-based. You pick your program when you sign up, and your homepage, search and dashboard only show the courses assigned to that program — so you are never scrolling past material you do not study. You can change your program from your dashboard.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. StudyCore works in any modern mobile browser — nothing to install. Videos and documents open inside the app, so notes and past papers are readable on a small screen, and your progress follows you when you switch to a laptop.

Do I need to download anything to read the notes?

No. PDFs and Word documents are rendered by the built-in StudyCore document viewer, with zoom, search and page navigation. Content stays view-only inside the platform.

What happens when my trial ends?

Your account, progress and streaks stay exactly as they are. Locked resources simply prompt you to upgrade, and any lesson marked as a free preview stays open. Upgrading at any time restores full access instantly.
