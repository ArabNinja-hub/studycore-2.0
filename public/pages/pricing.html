How to pay

Payments are made by mobile money and confirmed by the StudyCore team. After you send the payment, submit your reference from your dashboard — once confirmed, Premium activates immediately.

Payment questions

Good to know before you pay

How soon does Premium activate?

As soon as the StudyCore team confirms your payment reference. Approvals are done by hand, so it usually takes minutes rather than seconds — you get a confirmation email the moment access is granted, and your dashboard shows Premium as active.

Does it renew automatically?

No. Premium is 30 days of access, not a subscription. Nothing is ever deducted from your mobile money account automatically — when the 30 days are up you simply pay again if you want to continue.

I paid but Premium is still locked. What now?

First check that you submitted the payment reference from your dashboard — the payment and the account are matched by that reference. If it is submitted and still pending after a while, message the team on WhatsApp with the reference and the number you paid from, and it will be sorted out.

Can someone else pay for me?

Yes. The payment can come from any MTN MoMo or Airtel Money number — just submit the reference from your own StudyCore account so Premium is activated on the right profile.

Does Premium cover every course?

Premium unlocks all video lessons, notes, tutorial sheets and past papers available to your program — one price, no per-course charges. Your homepage still stays filtered to your program so you only see what you actually study.

What do I lose if I do not upgrade?

Nothing you have already done. Your account, completed lessons, streaks and achievements stay. Video lessons stay locked, and after the 30-day trial documents lock too — except lessons marked as free previews.
