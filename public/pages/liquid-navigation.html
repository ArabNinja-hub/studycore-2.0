How the liquid effect works

  • Base block. The navigation element is your position: relative; width: 400px; height: 70px flex container, centred with a soft shadow and rounded corners.
  • Goo filter. An inline SVG feGaussianBlur + feColorMatrix (the classic "goo" recipe) is applied only to the indicator layer.
  • Morphing blob. A main rounded pill plus two trailing blobs are translated to the active item; the goo filter melts them into one stretching droplet as they move.
  • Spring motion. A cubic-bezier ease and a short is-moving state scale the trailing blobs for a subtle wobble.
