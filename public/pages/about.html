About StudyCore

The home of your university learning

StudyCore is a modern university learning platform. We bring courses, lessons, study notes, video learning, past papers and revision resources together in one place — so students can learn smarter and stay on track.

What StudyCore is

StudyCore is built around one idea: a student's learning should live in one trusted place. Instead of scattered notes, video links and exam PDFs, every course follows the same clear structure — course, topic, lesson — with video lessons, study notes and past papers attached to each topic.

The platform is student-focused: you can track your progress across courses and topics, continue exactly where you left off, build study streaks and earn academic achievements as you complete lessons.

New students start with a 30-day free trial that includes access to study notes and documents. Video lessons are Premium content, available to StudyCore Premium students across every course.
