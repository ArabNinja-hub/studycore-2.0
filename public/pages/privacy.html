Privacy Policy

This policy describes the personal information StudyCore collects and how it is used. It applies to studycore.academy.

1. Information We Collect

StudyCore collects only the information needed to run your account and provide the learning platform. This includes:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile information: optional details you choose to provide, such as school, grade, and learning level.
  • Profile picture: if you upload one, stored and shown only for your account.
  • Learning activity: which lessons you have completed, your video playback position, the resources you have bookmarked, and when you last studied.
  • Subscription information: your trial and Premium status, and — when you request a subscription — the phone number and payment reference you provide.
  • Technical information: your device, browser, and IP address, collected automatically to secure the service and improve performance.
  • Cookies: an authentication cookie that keeps you signed in (see below).

2. How Information Is Used

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account and keep it secure.
  • Provide courses, lessons, notes, videos and past papers.
  • Track and display your learning progress, streaks and achievements.
  • Save your video position so you can resume where you left off.
  • Provide personalised features such as "continue learning" and recommendations.
  • Process and confirm subscriptions and payments.
  • Communicate important announcements and support you technically.
  • Protect against fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies

StudyCore uses a single authentication (session) cookie, stored in an httpOnly form so it cannot be read by scripts in your browser. It exists only to keep you signed in and to determine what you are authorised to access. StudyCore does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

4. Data Sharing

StudyCore does not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary:

  • Cloud storage: uploaded files (such as documents, videos and your profile picture) are stored with our cloud storage provider, Cloudflare.
  • Payments: when you subscribe, the payment reference and phone number you provide are used to confirm the payment. StudyCore does not store full card or bank details.
  • Legal authorities: where we are legally required to disclose information.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are currently paid by mobile money (for example MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money) and confirmed by the StudyCore team. StudyCore stores the payment reference and the phone number you supply for confirmation, but does not store raw card numbers or full payment-provider credentials.

6. Data Security

StudyCore uses reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including password hashing, secure session handling, and server-side access control over your content. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We keep your personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect security. You may request deletion of your account and associated data (see below).

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact StudyCore using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

StudyCore is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

10. Third-Party Services

StudyCore uses the following third-party services, each of which may process some of your information in line with its own privacy policy:

  • Cloudflare R2 — cloud storage for uploaded files.
  • Mobile money providers — used when you make a payment.
  • WhatsApp — an external channel we link to for announcements and community.

11. International Data Transfers

Some of the services StudyCore relies on (in particular cloud storage) may process or store data outside Zambia. When that happens, the data is handled under the provider's privacy and security policies.

12. Privacy Contact
